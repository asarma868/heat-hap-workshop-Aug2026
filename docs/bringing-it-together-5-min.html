Discussion questions for the group:

  1. Which of these three estimates would you trust most, and why? Is your answer different if you imagine this were real data versus knowing, as we do here, the actual ground truth?
  2. The case-crossover comparison is the closest analogue in this workshop to what a simple pre/post design (like the core of the Ahmedabad HAP evaluation) can offer. What would you need to add to make it a genuine causal comparison?
  3. The IPW step only adjusts for compositional drift we measured and simulated on purpose. What are two or three plausible unmeasured confounders, in either Boston or Ahmedabad, that no amount of reweighting on the variables we have could fix?
  4. If you had to design a real evaluation of a new heat-adaptation program with only 2 years of pre-data and 2 years of post-data, which of these three designs would you prioritize building the data infrastructure for, and why?
